The Art & Craft of Gujarat

Bandhani (Tie & Dye)

Originating from Jamnagar and Kutch, Bandhani involves plucking cloth into thousands of tiny bindings to create breathtaking geometric dot patterns.

Mirror Work (Abhla)

Traditional artisan craft from Kutch where tiny reflecting mirrors are intricately hand-stitched, sparkling dynamically under festive lights.

Block Printing

Ajrakh and Bagh block printing use natural vegetable dyes carved on wooden blocks, passed down through generations of master artisans.

Worn During Grand Celebrations

Gujarati traditional attire comes alive during vibrant festivals like Navratri (Garba), Uttarayan (Kite Festival), and grand Gujarati weddings filled with folk music and dance.
